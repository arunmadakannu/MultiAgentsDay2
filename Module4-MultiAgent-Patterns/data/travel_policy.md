# Contoso Global – Employee Travel & Expense Policy (v3.2)

## Section 1 – Submitting a claim
1.1 Claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the last expense date.
1.2 An itemised receipt is required for every expense **above $25**.
1.3 The same receipt must never be claimed twice. Duplicate receipts are rejected and reported to the manager.
1.4 The claimed total must equal the sum of the receipts. Claims with a calculation error are **sent back** to the employee for correction.

## Section 2 – Hotels
2.1 **Tier-1 cities** (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, New York, London, Singapore): maximum **$250 per night**.
2.2 **All other cities**: maximum **$150 per night**.
2.3 Any amount above the nightly limit is not reimbursed. The employee pays the difference.
2.4 Minibar, room service alcohol and in-room movies are not reimbursable.

## Section 3 – Meals
3.1 Meal allowance is **$60 per day** (breakfast, lunch and dinner combined).
3.2 **Alcohol is never reimbursable**, including when it is part of a meal bill.
3.3 Client entertainment meals need the client's name and company on the claim.

## Section 4 – Transport
4.1 Flights must be **economy class** for journeys under 6 hours.
4.2 Taxis and ride-share are reimbursable for business travel. Receipts above $25 are required.
4.3 Personal car mileage is paid at **$0.50 per mile**.

## Section 5 – Approval
5.1 Claims up to **$1,000** can be approved by the Finance Expense team.
5.2 Claims **above $1,000** also need the employee's **line manager** approval.
5.3 Any claim with a policy violation must list each violation and the amount removed.
